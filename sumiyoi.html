墨宵🌸桜と友達！！

Sumiyoi Sakura and Friends!!

a new adventure awaits for the upbringing of belonging..



following her departure from the manga world, Sumiyoi Sakura -- primarily known as the Princess of the Sumiyoi Clan who ruled the northern portion of a city in Japan -- has been tasked with taking care of a group of so-called "Unfortunates" who had their lives in complete disarray, often through sexual upheavals.


originating from a mysterious snail mail containing the profiles of the "Unfortunates" and their last known location, Sakura must try her absolute best to find and nurture the individuals before their lives begin to turn for the worst.


up north across the Hokkaido region, a mysterious campus stands tall; awaiting to receive the pact..

revitalizing the stories through the lens of the Unfortunates


what initially started as an action plan to revive the Metamorphisan legacy following Yoshida Saki's demise and a new breath of life, the project has emerged to a collective of its own -- adjoining the Unfortunates' circumstances to come up with a unified resolution.


the real task is what will be the strategies Sakura must impose in order to ensure that not only the Unfortunates get their second chances to right their own wrongs, but to also live without fear of upheavals getting in their way.
