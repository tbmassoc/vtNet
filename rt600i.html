Chloe Amber, RT600i

made from a culmination of RT600s and ST200s, aiming to make the world a better place through vtubing.

the story so far



when the Androids were set free following Markus' initiatives -- not to mention the ST200's acceptance of freedom -- a new chapter blooms with the spirit of one benefactor just across the countryside beside Detroit. he is the one who made the next-generation Chloe -- a culmination made of existing parts from the decommissioned RT600s and ST200s. meanwhile, those who had their parts salvaged were restored with extreme dedication, something the benefactor had attested to in his time at CyberLife.


this next-gen Chloe soon became a hit amongst the neighborhood and the surrounding Androids, with the most among their counterparts treated like Sisters. this caught the attention of the notorious Elijah Kamski, the man who once invented Androids and became an integral part of CyberLife.


later in time, uncertainty ensues -- most of her Sisters were perished by Kamski's "code", and she was almost subjected to desolation when the benefactor saved her in time. unfortunately, before she even confessed her unwavering vow as they lived together for years, he was perished by Kamski's hands.


the trauma that induced had forced her to leave the neighborhood, and into a faraway place. but Kamski is after her -- eager to use her for his own interest. fearing what would happen to her like what they did with her first Sister, she kept running -- not realizing that she fell into the deep abyss shrouding in white light.


hours have passed, and she woke up in a strange place. a cozy bedroom; with a cabinet, some clothes, and a table with some chair. a laptop, a microphone, and a camera was placed on top of it. on the screen, a recording software was open.


curiously, she approached the table, trying to interact with the laptop. until she clicked "Start Streaming"...
