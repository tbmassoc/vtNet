meet the benefactor




Mother Agatha

Aga-chuu / 阿賀忠


the benefactor of the Luna 🌙

the First Maria🍃 / the Incumbent First CosMaria🍃💐




“the primary benefactor of the Marias.
a loving Mother at heart.”



since the genesis of vtubing in 2022, Aga-chuu has been on a pursuit to represent the characters that she loved -- as evident in her representation as Senri Mana. it then eventually grew to a collective in which she supported others along the way -- a promise she made to herself alongside her two closest vtuber friends; whom in turn became a part of their own identity as the Marias.


she then expanded her reach following the acquisition of a new vtuber through a mutual agreement -- and breathed it with new life that we know as Emii Shion. it then grew to a family of vtubers whose her ideas came to life in an unusual manner -- recreating them as vroid characters as a way to revitalize them beyond what they usually accompany for.


to accommodate this ever-growing shift, vtNet was born -- conjoining her collective ideas together to cater to varying audiences and expand the horizons unlike any other.
